```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Design one new original identity concept for ЕТОР, a Russian investment-development umbrella brand. Apply the financial-sector lesson of a highly consistent masterbrand that can flex across audiences, with one distinctive graphic primitive. The overall design should feel precise, intelligent and current, with greater typographic finesse and more space than a heavy industrial logo.
One single actual landscape investor-guide cover, front-on, 3:2, as flat graphic art. No collage, no multiple options, no mockup, no brand guidelines grid. No borrowing of any recognizable existing bank logo.

Create a strong ORIGINAL compact emblem based on a shared open oval: two broad, precisely balanced curved strokes face each other and connect through one short central horizontal bar, forming a subtle abstract combination of Cyrillic Е and the oval О. The result is a crisp one-color typographic mark with a memorable countershape, not an infinity sign, not a chain, not a generic link, not a hexagon, not a planet. It must have a purposeful asymmetry and read as a solid symbol at small size, no fine lines inside the logo.
Pair this symbol with the exact uppercase Cyrillic word "ЕТОР" in an original medium-bold modern grotesque: moderate width, open shapes, finely controlled spacing, a recurring curved-to-flat transition that relates to the emblem. Final Р looks like Latin P and has no diagonal leg. The complete lockup goes in the upper left at a confident but measured size, approximately 37% page width.

Palette: deep ink teal #173C48, cool pale cyan #D7EEF0 and warm off-white #F7F5EF. Use the warm off-white for most of the background, ink for all text, and a single purposeful cyan graphic region on the right. Do not add another accent color.
The graphic region is an enormous cropped version of the emblem's open oval geometry entering from the right edge. Keep the geometry utterly flat, with ink and cyan planes nested into an open oval-shaped field and wide breathing room. The negative space should connect naturally to the empty editorial left side. No random background geometry, no all-over pattern. This graphic primitive should plausibly frame content and be repeated consistently in future family members.

Under the small brand lockup set exactly:
"Единая территория"
"опережающего развития"
At lower left set the publication title exactly, on two lines:
"Путеводитель"
"инвестора"
The title should be significantly smaller than a billboard headline and sit comfortably within generous margins. The composition is mature, beautifully restrained and distinctive, while the logo remains easy to isolate and reproduce.

No extra text, no English, no concept numbers, no subbrand names, no taglines, no percentages, charts, coins, credit cards, arrows, shields, government seals, KRDV logos, maps or landscapes. No gradients, glow, texture, shadows, 3D, perspective or glass effects. Crisp vector-like graphic finish, exact Russian spelling, exceptional optical balance.
```
