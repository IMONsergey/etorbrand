```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Design a new original identity concept for ЕТОР, a Russian investment-development umbrella brand. Apply lessons from sophisticated financial-sector identities: typographic authority, controlled visual hierarchy, a memorable proprietary detail, and restraint. Do NOT reproduce any existing bank logo, brand color combination, mascot or layout.
One single beautifully designed landscape investor-guide cover, perfectly front-on, approximately 3:2. It is one actual designed surface, not a collage, moodboard, mockup or logo variation sheet.

Create an exceptional bespoke Cyrillic wordmark reading exactly "ЕТОР". Use a modern, substantial medium-contrast serif with short incisive wedge terminals and generous open counters. The lettering must be contemporary and structurally confident, not old-fashioned, aristocratic, decorative or fashion-fragile. Each letter optically refined. A distinctive little wedge-cut junction shared by Е, Т and Р, and an unusually graceful slightly wide О. Final letter Cyrillic Р is shaped like Latin P, not Latin R. Make this medium-large wordmark the leading element at the upper-left, occupying around 42% of the page width, with generous margins. No separate heraldic symbol.

Palette: rich oxblood #552735 and warm ivory #F5F0E7, with a tiny dusty terracotta #BB7869 accent if useful. The background is warm ivory. The type is oxblood.
On the right half, a single restrained oversized cropped oval line motif derived from the О bowl: 10-14 thin parallel curved outlines with a single purposeful opening, all in oxblood, drawing a quiet sense of connected space. The line motif remains flat, understated and leaves extensive negative space. It is a design motif, not a chart, map or security engraving. Avoid a rosette, floral pattern, infinity symbol, swoosh or spinning vortex.

Set the full descriptor below the wordmark in a small, excellent neutral sans serif, exactly on two lines:
"Единая территория"
"опережающего развития"
At lower left, the publication title in restrained medium-size sans serif, exactly on two lines:
"Путеводитель"
"инвестора"
Typography hierarchy: wordmark prominent but NOT gigantic; descriptor small; publication title medium. Large quiet center, editorial poise. Premium institutional credibility that remains accessible, not exclusive wealth management.
No other text, no English, no fake slogans, no government emblems, no KRDV branding, no coins, bank cards, interest rates or dollar signs. No drop shadows, gradients, 3D, glossy objects, decorative textures, borders around the whole page, or mockup perspective. Extremely clean original graphic design.
```
