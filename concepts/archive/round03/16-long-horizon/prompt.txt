TITLE: "16 / Длинный горизонт"
RATIONALE exactly: "Бренд сопровождает проект на всем пути."
SIGN DESIGN: A unique horizontal compact symbol composed of a long calm solid bar and one short vertical notch that changes the level of its last quarter. One upper edge stays absolutely continuous, the lower edge has a precisely proportioned step. It should read like a proprietary horizon glyph, not an equals sign, minus sign, arrow or letter E. Exceptionally simple, with no additional decoration.
TYPOGRAPHY: Understated beautifully spaced medium-light neo-grotesque. Editorial confidence, not monumental heaviness.
PALETTE: Ink #182B35, bright azure #399ADD, pure white #FAFCFD.
VISUAL SYSTEM: A persistent horizon line aligns headings across a family of documents, with one unique stepped edge creating recognizability. Spacious and quiet.
APPLICATIONS: a large editorial document spread reading «Путь проекта» with four short stage words and a calm architectural entrance fascia
LAYOUT: Use a predominantly white sophisticated brand board with a wide top hero occupying 40%, a lower large editorial application occupying 40%, and a narrow supporting panel showing the second application and one-color sign. Grid aligned, no mechanical divider lines, no excessive microtext.
