TITLE: "15 / Связность"
RATIONALE exactly: "Согласованные действия создают прочную связь."
SIGN DESIGN: A compact original woven joint formed by exactly two broad flat strips crossing at a right angle with one intentional over-under cut. Architectural and minimal, not a Celtic knot, infinity sign, chain or decorative braid. The negative cut creates a precise unique square aperture and the silhouette is asymmetrical, with four unequal ends.
TYPOGRAPHY: Contemporary slightly condensed grotesque with calm confident medium weight.
PALETTE: Dark plum #3C2148, vivid rose #F578B4, white #FCF8FC.
VISUAL SYSTEM: The over-under relationship becomes a graphic rule for image/text intersections and layered labels. Keep it flat, editorial and restrained.
APPLICATIONS: a cultural-level sophisticated investor event poster and an investor guide with a woven graphic edge
LAYOUT: Use a bold architectural presentation: a tall richly colored panel on the left 32% carries an inverted logo and one concise rationale. The white right 68% contains a large primary application, a secondary application, and small sign/palette/type proofs. Keep the actual symbol large enough to judge and type airy.
