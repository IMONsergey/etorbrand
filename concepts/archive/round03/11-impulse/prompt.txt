TITLE: "11 / Импульс"
RATIONALE exactly: "Новые условия меняют траекторию проекта."
SIGN DESIGN: One continuous medium-bold line with an unusual controlled phase shift: horizontal entry, a short sharp rise, then a long calm horizontal exit at a new level. Fold it into a compact distinctive rectangular glyph without arrowheads. Not an ECG pulse, not a stock rising graph, no lightning bolt. Flat, precise, energetic but composed.
TYPOGRAPHY: Modern technical sans with open forms, medium weight, restrained condensed headings.
PALETTE: Electric purple #603BFA, charcoal #202026, clean white #FAF9FD.
VISUAL SYSTEM: A single shift in baseline organizes photography, headings and tabs. Energy comes from one intentional displacement rather than repeated decoration.
APPLICATIONS: a contemporary event poster reading «Новые возможности для проектов» and an investor portal homepage with a single elegant photographic strip
LAYOUT: Use a bold architectural presentation: a tall richly colored panel on the left 32% carries an inverted logo and one concise rationale. The white right 68% contains a large primary application, a secondary application, and small sign/palette/type proofs. Keep the actual symbol large enough to judge and type airy.
