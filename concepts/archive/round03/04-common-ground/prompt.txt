TITLE: "04 / Общая основа"
RATIONALE exactly: "Разным проектам нужна понятная общая основа."
SIGN DESIGN: A compact sign composed of two equal-length horizontal bars and a single short vertical bridge between them, positioned off-center. The exact negative spaces make it a custom stable symbol that hints at both an equals sign and a structural joint. Avoid a literal standard equals glyph, hashtag, currency sign or barcode. Restrained and intellectually strong.
TYPOGRAPHY: Precise neutral medium-weight grotesque with round O and distinctive narrow Cyrillic Р.
PALETTE: Deep pine #164B42, bright mint #B2F1C9, clean white #F5F8F5.
VISUAL SYSTEM: Parallel rules organize dense content. Constant alignment holds varied content blocks together; calm, exceptionally clear information design.
APPLICATIONS: a modern investor-guide spread headed «Условия участия» with three large category cards and no invented figures, and a folder cover
