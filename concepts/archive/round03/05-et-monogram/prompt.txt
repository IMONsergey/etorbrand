RATIONALE exactly: "Собственный знак рождается из имени ЕТОР."
SIGN DESIGN: A refined original Cyrillic ЕТ monogram. It should look like one elegant purpose-drawn glyph, not an extra-bold E and not two overlapping typed letters. No retro ligatures, stencil gaps, shield or rectangle enclosure.

PALETTE: Black #161619, saturated violet #7449F8, pale lilac-white #F7F5FC.
VISUAL SYSTEM: The monogram's junction gives a distinctive page corner and editorial crop. The wordmark and sign should feel designed together.
