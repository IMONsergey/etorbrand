TITLE: "01 / Сборка"
RATIONALE exactly: "Разные проекты складываются в общую систему."
SIGN DESIGN: Three complementary polygonal pieces, deliberately unequal, fit tightly into a compact near-square silhouette. A precise shared negative-space T appears at their junction. No separate square grid, no cube, no puzzle knobs. One piece has a single distinctive bevel. The shared space, not three separate icons, is the memorable feature.
TYPOGRAPHY: Calm contemporary medium-weight slightly condensed grotesque; generous optical spacing.
PALETTE: Cobalt #2548F4, near-black #151A23, cool-white #F5F7FC.
VISUAL SYSTEM: The three fitted pieces become interchangeable content panels with a constant shared joint; show that the same geometry accommodates different proportions.
APPLICATIONS: a sophisticated editorial investor-guide cover and a large exhibition wall featuring one full-bleed contemporary industrial photograph
LAYOUT: Use an asymmetric editorial board: left 42% is a generous white hero zone with the large sign+wordmark, descriptor, one concise rationale; right 58% contains two generous application panels stacked with one small graphic-system detail.
