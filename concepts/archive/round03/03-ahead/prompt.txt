TITLE: "03 / Опережение"
RATIONALE exactly: "Общая система помогает проекту двигаться дальше."
SIGN DESIGN: Three precise parallel short diagonal solid bands form a compact dynamic glyph. The middle band advances forward by a measured step, creating a distinctive negative-space break. NO arrowhead, no speed swoosh, no sports slash logo. The silhouette must be balanced and intelligible in flat one-color form, not a stock equalizer.
TYPOGRAPHY: Purpose-designed slightly wide contemporary sans, medium weight, no italic wordmark.
PALETTE: Midnight #111C38, electric cyan #53D7F5, white #F8FCFF.
VISUAL SYSTEM: A measured offset between aligned panels expresses forward movement. Develop the exact offset into crops and page transitions, no decorative waves.
APPLICATIONS: a panoramic event screen reading «Территория развития» and an elegant information booklet with precise chapter tabs
LAYOUT: Use a bold architectural presentation: a tall richly colored panel on the left 32% carries an inverted logo and one concise rationale. The white right 68% contains a large primary application, a secondary application, and small sign/palette/type proofs. Keep the actual symbol large enough to judge and type airy.
