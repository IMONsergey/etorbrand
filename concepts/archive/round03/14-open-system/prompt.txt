TITLE: "14 / Открытая система"
RATIONALE exactly: "Система сохраняет единство и допускает расширение."
SIGN DESIGN: A simple open polygonal contour made from five robust straight segments, with one open corner and a short continuation extending beyond that corner. The distinctive open-ended topology should suggest extensibility without becoming a bracket frame, a link or a generic broken circle. One coherent line, carefully calibrated lengths.
TYPOGRAPHY: Humanist contemporary sans, rounded internal counters, clear normal weight.
PALETTE: Rich blue #1769E9, ultralight blue #DDEDFE, graphite #1E2530.
VISUAL SYSTEM: The open contour expands around different editorial content and continues off the edge. A recognizable open corner anchors every application.
APPLICATIONS: a spacious website information page and a pair of document covers titled «Путеводитель инвестора» and «Материалы проекта»
LAYOUT: Use a horizontal editorial composition: upper 44% holds a beautifully composed main logo at left and a distinctive system sample at right; lower 56% has two large application panels with unequal widths.
