Use case: logo-brand. Design a clear, professional, contemporary identity presentation slide for ЕТОР, the Russian investment-development brand. 16:9 landscape, one concept. The client explicitly rejected strange abstract signs, invented symbolism, broken geometry and awkward custom letterforms. This time simplicity, familiarity and typographic quality are essential.

LOGO, EXACTLY: a perfectly regular solid vermilion-red circle containing one large clearly readable WHITE CYRILLIC CAPITAL Е. Draw Е as an ordinary well-proportioned geometric sans-serif letter: one straight vertical stem, three perfectly horizontal bars, middle bar a little shorter. No cuts, gaps, ligatures, tilted elements, shadows, perspective, unusual joins, added shapes or deconstruction. This is a simple familiar letter-based symbol. Next to the circle, write ЕТОР in a beautifully spaced normal medium-weight contemporary sans-serif similar in character to Onest or Manrope. Standard upright Cyrillic capitals, never stretched, never stylized. The final Р has no diagonal leg. The circle is only about 1.2 times the cap height of the wordmark. Give a comfortable gap between circle and wordmark. The entire logo must look balanced, precise and effortless.

PALETTE: vermilion #F04432, near-black #202125, clean white #FAFAF8. Exactly these visual roles, no gradients. Predominantly white. Contemporary financial-sector clarity and editorial typography. No retro, no serif, no industrial stencil.

SLIDE: refined designer presentation, not a technical guideline grid. Upper 45%: very generous white space, main logo clearly visible at left. Descriptor underneath in small regular type, exactly two lines: «Единая территория» / «опережающего развития». Small running header «ЕТОР / ВАРИАНТ 01». At upper right, a standalone red circular sign at a smaller scale, one black monochrome version, three small color chips, and type sample «Аа Бб 0123». Do not add any conceptual slogans or explanations. No divider lines and no giant graphic shapes around the hero.
Lower 55%: TWO beautifully designed realistic flat application layouts, comfortably separated and aligned. LEFT, 60% width: a clean desktop website hero, directly viewed, NO laptop or browser frame. White background, exact small logo top-left, restrained navigation «О проекте», «Условия», «Контакты». Headline «Условия для развития вашего проекта» in a clear medium-weight grotesque. One small red rectangular button «Узнать больше». Right half of the website has a calm editorial photograph of a bright modern industrial building, ordinary rectangular crop, no decorative clipped corners or waves. RIGHT, 40% width: flat front cover of a document. Solid red background, small inverted logo top-left, large white title «Путеводитель инвестора» at bottom-left, perfectly aligned. One enlarged subtle white circle cropped at the far right edge can be the sole graphic device. No shadows, perspective or staged book.
Use the exact same normal circular Е sign and medium-weight ЕТОР on every application. The point is an ordinary, strong, understandable mark executed with excellent proportions and design quality. No new abstract symbol, no made-up cleverness, no fancy unnecessary geometry, no KRDV identity, no other logos, no text mistakes, no extra labels, no watermark.
