## Точный текст запроса

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one exceptionally art-directed graphic identity concept for the Russian investment development brand ЕТОР, a standalone institutional brand intended to develop into an umbrella family. This is an original identity exploration, not related visually to KRDV. Design a single actual landscape investor-guide cover, seen perfectly front-on and flat. This is one coherent designed surface, not a moodboard, not a collage, not a sheet of multiple options or mockups. Landscape aspect ratio about 3:2, high resolution, impeccable crisp vector-like graphic rendering.

Concept: the four-letter Cyrillic name itself is the identity. Make the EXACT word "ЕТОР" the dominant central element, occupying roughly 75% of the cover width. Draw original custom lettering rather than an ordinary font: robust wide modern grotesque, substantial weight, optically refined spacing, square-cut structural terminals, subtly softened inner corners, deliberate relationship of the Е horizontal strokes, the Т crossbar, and the squared-round О and Р bowls. One memorable shared construction detail through the letters; strongly legible, no extra letters, no stencil fragmentation, no italic racing or gaming style. The final Cyrillic Р looks like Latin P, NOT Latin R. No separate emblem.

Palette proposal: warm almost-white background #F4F2EC, deep graphite lettering #202321, one clean vermilion-orange accent #F04A24. Bold but credible for long-term investment and infrastructure, not a bank template. High typographic sophistication and optical balance. Use wide generous margins and an asymmetric editorial composition with about half the surface left quiet. Extend a single measured orange horizontal graphic gesture from the Е construction toward the outer edge, as a restrained identity motif and composition anchor, integrated below the wordmark; avoid arrowheads. Place small, impeccably typeset Cyrillic descriptor aligned to the wordmark, with exactly these two lines:
"Единая территория"
"опережающего развития"
At the lower left, one modest publication title, exactly:
"Путеводитель инвестора"
No additional text, no English, no concept numbers, no color swatches, no annotations, no tagline, no other brands, no government emblems. Strictly flat design, no perspective, no paper mockup, no gradients, no lighting effects, no shadows, no 3D, no landscapes, no maps, no decorative pictograms. The result should feel like an original identity by an excellent typography-led design studio, not a stock corporate template.
```
