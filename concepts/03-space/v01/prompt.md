```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original refined graphic identity exploration for the Russian investment development umbrella brand ЕТОР. Deliver a single actual flat investor-guide cover in landscape, approximately 3:2 aspect ratio, front-on, crisp graphic design. One coherent composition only. No collage, no multi-panel board, no array of logo variations, no 3D mockup.

Concept: territory as an open, precisely organized space in which a project can grow. Design a proprietary simple geometric emblem from two offset, substantial right-angle strokes. Together they define an open square field, with one intentional generous opening at the upper-right. The negative space should subtly suggest a broad Т-like junction. Avoid literal arrows, a house roof, a location pin, a scanning icon, a real-estate facade, or crop marks. Create a balanced compact single-color mark with confident weight and unusually good proportions, able to stand alone.

Place that emblem with the exact word "ЕТОР" as a clear medium-large horizontal logo lockup in the upper left, occupying about half the page width. The wordmark must use original proportionally elegant Cyrillic grotesque lettering with open counters and a restrained distinctive detail; not a conventional thin font, not futuristic. Last letter is Cyrillic Р shaped like Latin P, never Latin R.

On the right, expand the same open-field principle into one enormous cropped emerald outline, with its open gap clearly visible. It is a graphic composition device derived from the small emblem, not another unrelated illustration. Keep the center completely empty and spacious. No modular grid wallpaper. Align all elements precisely. Make the typography and negative space do most of the work.

Palette proposal: very light cool mint #E8F1E9 background, very deep forest green #124A3B lettering and main outline, a small restrained fresh green #70C49A accent at one structural junction. Mature, architectural, composed, confident. Flat solid colors only.

Under the logo lockup set exactly:
"Единая территория"
"опережающего развития"
At the bottom left set exactly:
"Путеводитель инвестора"

No other text, no English, no concept names, no subbrand names, no numbers, no taglines, no other logos, no government heraldry or KRDV visual elements. No landscapes, maps, bridges, suns, swooshes, arrows, gradients, texture, drop shadows, glass or 3D. A polished ownable identity cover, not a generic template or a technology dashboard.
```
